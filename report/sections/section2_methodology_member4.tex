\subsection{Adam and AdEMAMix}\label{sec:method-m4}
\owner{Member 4}

Adam keeps moving averages of gradients and squared gradients, applies bias correction,
and uses the resulting coordinate-wise update \citep{kingma2015adam}.
AdEMAMix adds a slower gradient moving average to retain longer history alongside the
fast moving average \citep{pagliardini2025ademamix}.
We will test whether the additional history helps at the relatively short training horizons
feasible on CPUs. The comparison uses zero weight decay for both methods. This isolates the
history mechanism from differences between Adam and AdamW regularization. A no-slow-EMA
ablation will use otherwise matched settings.
